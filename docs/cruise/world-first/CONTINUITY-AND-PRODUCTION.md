# Continuity and production limits

The first target remains one convincing 60-second night drive. A saved 60-second camera sequence is not a finished film. A player that changes decoder slots is not evidence that independently authored roads join convincingly.

## What the local experiment can establish

The founder diagnostic contains two contiguous 135-frame clips, 4.5 seconds each, at 30 fps. Their boundary preserves source-frame ordering: frame134 is followed by frame135, with no repeated connector. This can test decoder preloading and radio independence. It cannot validate branching, a fictional city's continuation, optical compositing, or the reference's last-to-first loop. Browser measurements belong in the QA evidence, including any exposed black frame, delay or pause.

For authored native segments, retain one continuous camera/road animation and split the encoded output at frame boundaries. Match the camera transform, velocity, acceleration, grade, lane position, exposure, traffic phase and visible lighting across the cut. Keep radio outside both decoder slots. The current 720m route ends at a different position and elevation from its beginning; it must not loop.

Independent branching is substantially harder: every foreground object, traffic vehicle, reflection, exposure state and destination-visible street must agree at the common boundary. No seamless branching is demonstrated here. A longer continuously authored film with fewer transitions is the appropriate first production experiment. A fade to black would hide a restart rather than establish world continuity.

## Measured diagnostic delivery

The two founder clips total1,184,560bytes over9seconds: approximately1.053Mbit/s of video payload. They are VP8,368x496, silent and reference-only. This number must not be used as the bitrate estimate for photographic1080p output. Container overhead, transfer behavior, caching and radio traffic are separate measurements.

For a measured delivery bitrate B in Mbit/s, video payload is approximately7.5B MB for60seconds,37.5B MB for5minutes and300B MB for40minutes. These are duration formulas, not budgets or measured production file sizes. An extended session scales with its actual delivered duration; repeat playback may be cached but does not create new world content.

The actual native diagnostic now contains60 PNG frames,1920x1080 at30fps, totaling97,481,942bytes. The successful MRQ script took58.031seconds, with rendering beginning28.906seconds after script entry: the29.125-second queue interval includes PIE startup, warm-up and finalization. It is not a steady per-frame render rate. These PNGs are display-referred, not a linearEXR archival master.

Encoding and actual decoding validation took5.059seconds. Two30-frame VP8 clips total2,530,845bytes over2seconds, approximately10.123Mbit/s. Each clip has a1-second duration and no audio. This short, frequently keyed sample is not an optimized long-form delivery bitrate. A60-second/5-minute/40-minute file size remains unmeasured; use the duration formulas as scenarios only. H264 and physical Safari/device compatibility are not established by this VP8 result.

At30fps,60seconds requires1,800frames;5minutes9,000frames;40minutes72,000frames. Estimate render time only after a stable representative render has been measured, and report cold startup/shader compilation separately. A short warm-up-dominated benchmark does not justify a precise40-minute forecast. Disk capacity, failed-frame retries, mastering, grading, encoding and art production must also be accounted for.

Holding this diagnostic's average PNG size constant would require approximately2.9GB for60seconds,14.6GB for5minutes or117GB for40minutes. This is a storage scenario using the actual2-second sample, not an observed long render, anEXR estimate or a production budget. More detailed scenes can compress differently, and intermediate renders may coexist with final encodes. Disk space must be checked before long jobs.

Prepared video served by a CDN avoids rendering the world separately for each viewer. It still incurs video storage and transfer, plus production rendering. No CDN, paid service or cloud GPU was enabled here. Unreal Pixel Streaming is not part of this proof; no per-viewer GPU price is asserted.

## Sunset and advertising remain future production work

After Night passes its art gate, author a40-minute sequence with keyed sun/sky position, physically appropriate atmospheric changes, controlled exposure and staged street/building light activation across golden hour, sunset, twilight, blue hour and night. Render representative checkpoints before extending duration. A screen-color overlay does not establish that transition.

The Avenue remains hidden and its production infrastructure unchanged. Baked billboards cannot be edited at runtime. Future alternatives include prepared approved ad variants, separate perspective-tracked layers with occlusion masks, or calibrated realtime surfaces. None is proven by the current plate and no ownership, audience or revenue claim is introduced. Preserve the uninterrupted drive and radio before pursuing those layers.
