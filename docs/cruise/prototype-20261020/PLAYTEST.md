# Prototype playtest — October 20, 2026

This is a study plan, not completed research. Test the isolated prototype route and record its exact URL, commit, browser, device and date. Preserve the original `/cruise` experience. No participant outreach, accounts, payments or services are authorized by this document.

## People and format

Run 8–12 individual sessions of about 20 minutes, including 6–8 potential listeners and 2–4 independent shop, label, artist, station or event representatives. Avoid recruiting only project collaborators. Record overlapping roles and assistance explicitly. Use anonymous study labels; do not collect contact details or music profiles. Ask consent before notes, recording or voluntary diagnostics export. Participation and diagnostic collection are separate choices.

Begin with five unprompted minutes. Say “Use this as you would if someone sent you the link.” Do not explain the controls. Then give the tasks below. Separate unassisted success, success after a hint, failure and unavailable external dependency. An outage is not participant failure. Stop if requested.

## Listener tasks

1. Start listening; explain what Play does and whether you must drive manually. Record time to first audible music, retries and any scenery/audio delay separately.
2. Find a different station, adjust volume, pause and resume. Switch camera or vehicle and try listening without scenery. Record interruptions without assuming their cause.
3. Save a station and find it again. If fresh verified track metadata is genuinely available, save that song; otherwise ask what a broadcast bookmark means. Do not fabricate a live song to complete the task.
4. Find Phone & TV help. Explain which device renders and controls Cruise. Check whether the person mistakes instructions for a connected TV or paired remote.
5. Enable TV presentation; wait idle, recover controls, use the volume slider with keyboard/touch, open a dialog and leave a control focused. Verify that neither dialogs nor focused controls disappear.
6. Ask what they would return for and what stopped them from listening. A stated intention is not an observed return visit.

## Advertiser tasks

Before naming the directory, ask what businesses or signs they noticed. Then ask them to open Along the drive, inspect a business, distinguish HOUSE from DEMO, and return to listening. Explain that no sponsorship, purchase or property transfer is active. Ask whether the placement fits the city and what evidence would justify a small pilot. Record unaided recall separately from prompted card inspection; do not turn either into an attention guarantee.

## Physical phone/TV verification

Use actual hardware: at least one iPhone with Apple TV/AirPlay-compatible TV and one Android phone with a compatible Google streaming device. Record unavailable combinations instead of marking them passed. Follow the linked [Apple screen-mirroring instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102661) and [Google Home screen-casting instructions](https://support.google.com/chromecast/answer/6059461?hl=en). Test same-network discovery, connect, visible moving scene, audible radio, phone volume/tuning, orientation, six-second idle recovery, interruption and disconnect. Record OS/browser/Home versions, receiver model, observed latency, audio routing and failures. Also test desktop Chrome tab mirroring if equipment exists.

The source device still renders the scene. TV presentation changes the interface; it does not add graphics power. There is no native receiver, application-controlled device picker or paired phone remote. Separate devices opening the URL have separate sessions. Browser emulation and opening the help dialog do not validate TV hardware. Protect personal notifications during any screen recording.

## Measures and denominators

Report counts with denominators: unassisted Play successes / all task attempts; audible starts / attempted starts; station saves / listeners asked; verified song saves / listeners who actually encountered fresh verified metadata; TV connection successes / attempted eligible physical setups. Report excluded and assisted attempts alongside results. These small samples are directional, not population conversion estimates.

Local business JSON contains only the latest 100 DEMO / TEST observations in this browser's memory. Export with consent before leaving; note truncation. An opportunity is a continuous geometric readability qualification, not proven attention, a unique person or a billable impression. A deliberate world inspection and a directory inspection have different surfaces. Do not divide all inspections by world opportunities: directory inspections may have no preceding world opportunity. If analyzing world activations, show matched encounter counts and unmatched activations separately. Numbered Avenue drive-bys are a separate system.

## Next investment decision

Fix any blocked Play, lost audio, inaccessible volume, misleading connection claim or modal trap before expansion. If at least 75% of listener attempts start unaided (for example, 6/8 or 5/6), all tested input paths retain essential controls, and listeners describe a concrete reason to return, run a longer listening beta. Treat this as an experimental gate, not an industry benchmark. Use consenting repeat visits to test retention later. Pursue one manual sponsor pilot only if at least two representatives can accurately explain the demo and request a concrete follow-up; do not enable transactions. Invest in a native receiver or paired remote only after repeated observed mirroring friction and explicit demand; invest in wider scenery after the music loop and physical-device reliability meet these gates.
