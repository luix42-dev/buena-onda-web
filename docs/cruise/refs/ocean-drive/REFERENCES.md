# Ocean Drive accuracy references (Buena Onda Cruise)

Compiled 2026-10-13. Link-only index; no images are stored in the repo.

**Verification method.** License, author and date for every entry were read from the Wikimedia Commons file metadata (MediaWiki API `extmetadata`, the same data the file page shows) on 2026-10-13. Entries marked **[V]** were also opened and visually inspected, so their "shows" notes come from the pixels. Entries marked **[C]** have license and caption verified but I did not inspect the image; treat their detail notes as caption-only. File page URL = `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/` + the `File:` name below (spaces as underscores).

**Not used:** Fab "Beach City Modular Pack", stock sites, Google Street View, anything with unclear license. Hotel official sites were not fetched, so all facts below come from photos and captions only.

Facts for modeling:
- Stories: 3 upper floors over a ground-floor sidewalk cafe in the 2011 and 2019 photos. **Uncertain** whether to count that as 3 or 4 stories.
- Form: symmetrical, flat roof, low stepped wings either side of a central vertical pylon; thin horizontal eyebrow ledges at each floor; stripe panels beside the pylon; curved ledge under the HOTEL canopy; zig-zag painted band on the parapet (2011).
- Sign: vertical COLONY on two pylon faces with a central neon tube; horizontal HOTEL on a rounded canopy above the entrance. Tall narrow Deco letters with outlines.
- Neon at night: blue/cyan for sign, parapet strips and eyebrow edges; the whole facade reads blue. Neighbour Boulevard is red.
- Daytime colour by era: 2011 white/mint/pink; 2025 pale blue. **Uncertain** which is the 2026 state; no 2026 daytime Colony photo was found.

Confirmation / correction of our assumptions:
1. Lemon-yellow stucco: **partly wrong.** Walls are cream/white; yellow is accent only (fins, eyebrows, bands, rails). Only the central pylon panel is a pale yellow wall area.
2. White eyebrows: **wrong.** Eyebrows and canopy edges are bright yellow.
3. Vertical "LESLIE" fin: **wrong for today.** The fins are blank yellow fluted pylons; the name is small horizontal "LESLIE" over the entrance at the first-floor line, grey/silver letters, lit white at night.
4. Stories: ground floor plus 2 upper floors (two window rows above the entrance level in the 2021 photo), with a stepped central pylon and a rooftop flagpole. Counting convention uncertain.
5. Terrace: low yellow railing around the front terrace on a dark base, 3 centre entrance steps, orange umbrellas, orange/yellow awnings.
Uncertain: exact paint hex (pure saturated yellow trim, pale cream-yellow panel); JPEG colour varies with light.

Geography note (inference, not from a labelled photo): Ocean Drive runs north-south with the ocean to the east, so hotel faces point east and are in shade at sunset while the sky behind them (west) is the bright side.
