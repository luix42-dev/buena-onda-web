# Beach City reference folder (finish target)

Source: [Beach City - Modular Pack](https://www.fab.com/listings/c90bc1f1-3d5d-469e-a1d3-df2b439753f0) by PropHaus on Fab.
`gallery-01.jpg` … `gallery-23.jpg` are the listing's public gallery previews, downloaded 2026-10-13 at Luis's request.
They are third-party marketing images. They stay local and are gitignored (`.gitignore`). They are never shipped, committed or published.

Use them only as a "finish" target: lighting, grading, material richness, prop density and street dressing.
Compare them against matched Cruise frames to score the gaps in `docs/cruise/LOOK-BIBLE.md` §5.
Nothing from the pack is imported, traced or reproduced. That covers its meshes, textures, signage and layouts.
Accuracy references (real, licensed Ocean Drive photos) live in `../ocean-drive/REFERENCES.md`.
