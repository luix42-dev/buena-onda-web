These are application world units treated as metres, not surveyed geographic distances or a street-accurate Miami map.

The original has roughly **11 times** the previous Miami travel distance. It also changes road/environment families, rather than showing the same corridor at different camera angles. The expansion is **3 times** the previous Miami distance but still only **27%** of the Original's route length. Similar nominal lap times result from different existing speeds; they do not mean equivalent map size. No driving speed was reduced to manufacture a longer ride.

- Road, curb/sidewalk geometry, land and ocean continue to 1,130 m so their forward endpoint lies beyond the 420 m fog distance at the final driving position. Texture repeat density is preserved as the road extends.

## Bounded performance correction: implemented, final measurement pending

The lead's initial expanded-route measurement reported approximately 23.68 FPS for Mobile quality/night at 1440×900 in Chase, versus the earlier recorded 50.85 FPS. That result triggered one bounded correction; it does not isolate palms as the cause or establish that either run had identical external GPU contention.

Palm placements are now merged into **160 m spatial sections** instead of one route-wide batch. The same sixty placements and source geometry are retained. Each original material receives one shared sway-material clone across all sections; the existing component retains one animation clock. Each merged section computes a bounding sphere padded by 6 m for shader-driven palm motion, allowing normal Three.js frustum culling without manually hiding nearby scenery. Cleanup disposes each geometry and each unique owned material once. Sparse skyline batching and architecture are unchanged.

This trades additional mesh/draw-call opportunities for culling of off-camera palm sections; it is not free and is **not yet a demonstrated FPS improvement**. The current build passed according to the lead. The matched final benchmark is running after the user closed other previews, so that environmental change must be noted when interpreting comparisons. Do not attribute any eventual recovery solely to the partition without a controlled isolation run. No further source optimization is implied by this report.
