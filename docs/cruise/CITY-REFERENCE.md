# Scenic city corridor

The launch route uses the existing bounded pool: 36 modules × 64 metres, a 2,304 metre lap. South Beach occupies modules 0–8; the causeway 9–17; Downtown/Bayside 18–26; MiMo Boulevard 27–35. Existing district IDs remain usable. `SCENIC_DISTRICTS` exposes the four selectable destinations and their approach modules.

South Beach combines the existing authored deco hotels with pastel facades, a palm promenade, a western land parcel and eastern sand/water. The causeway keeps an unobstructed water span, parapets and piers. Downtown has a taller street wall, alternating eastern waterfront openings and an elevated cross street at module 23. MiMo uses low shop buildings, parking aprons, sloping roof accents and roadside sign pylons. The handoff modules carry the preceding family on their rear half and the next family on their forward half.

## Path and integration

`routeRelativePose(distance, longitudinalZ, lateralX = 0)` in `lib/cruise/routePath.ts` converts driving coordinates into the current road tangent frame. First wrap pooled positions with `moduleZ`; add the object's longitudinal offset before conversion. It returns `{x, z, yaw}`. The car remains at `HERO_X`, points down −Z and retains its driving-local camera anchors. `routeCurvature(distance)` supplies signed turning per metre for steering animation.

The path uses two periodic sine components. Position, tangent and curvature repeat every lap; there is no point where the hero orientation jumps. This is an endless periodic corridor with repeated scenery, rather than a geographic circle whose world coordinates close on themselves. World module geometry is prewarped relative to its own tangent, then each module moves into the current car frame. Long ground slabs and guardrails are split into overlapping eight metre pieces. Three continuous road ribbons reuse fixed buffers and resample eight metre cross sections each frame, avoiding hard 64 metre road corners.

Hotels and palms reuse existing authored geometry, instancing, asset loaders and disposal paths. Background bands rotate about their own longitudinal anchor in the same tangent frame. Module visibility uses a fixed bubble: 110 metres behind the car, with forward reaches of 250/330/440 metres for low/medium/high. Low quality reduces facade/palm detail. Low/medium keep two authored hotels per full block, high keeps three. Decorative and authored hotel/palm shadow passes are reserved for high; medium retains procedural building shadows. No runtime object spawning, render targets or per-frame material allocation is added. The road adds three fixed buffer geometries, with 289 samples each, and one shared center-line material; all are disposed on unmount.

## Sponsor parcels and measurement

The stable module IDs remain `coastal-approach` (module 5, records), `highway-open-20` (module 29, Branches) and `highway-billboard-run` (module 32, Tideway). Place/campaign IDs, destinations, sign creatives, configuration and discovery links stay intact. A reserved parcel clears nearby opaque frontage and accessories before deformation; each dimensional landmark and its approach apron then follows the same road tangent. The historic module names are compatibility anchors, not current district labels.

Place exposure retains its distance, facing, projected-area, frustum and two-second qualification checks. It additionally raycasts against marked opaque building batches, authored hotel parts and landmark shells at most four times per second. Up to three points across the sign are sampled. A sign blocked at all three points cannot qualify at that sample; qualification resets when it becomes blocked. Payloads explicitly say `estimated_visible_duration_occlusion_sampled`, `occlusion_tested: true`, and `occlusion_samples: 3`. This is sampled geometric visibility, not verified attention, verified impressions or pixel-level visibility. Foliage, the cabin, transparent details, screen overlays and unmarked background/skyline surfaces are not occluders in this check. Motion between samples can introduce up to 250 ms of visibility error; a partly visible sign can qualify. Hidden document tabs do not accrue time.

It confirmed 36 unique modules, all three stable place anchors, four district destinations and an exact driving-local hero lane. Maximum repeat-lap pose difference was `3.55e-13`; maximum composed module/asset pose versus direct pose difference was `9.09e-13`.
