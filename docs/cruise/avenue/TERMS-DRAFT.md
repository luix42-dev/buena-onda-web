## 1. What you are buying

1.1 A "plot" is a numbered display slot on the Buena Onda Cruise (a virtual street, "the Avenue"): for example a billboard, storefront sign, banner, roadside sign, bench or lamp-post flag.

1.2 Buying a plot gives you a **limited, revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence** to have your approved name, tagline, colour and link shown on that plot. It is not ownership of property, land, a domain, a token, a security or an investment. You get no intellectual-property rights in the Cruise, its artwork, its code or its street. **[LEGAL REVIEW]** Confirm the licence characterisation and that the "value"/"buyout" language cannot be read as an investment or a security.

1.3 Plots numbered as "house" plots (the Buena Onda Record Store, Branches Vintage, Vice Nights) are Buena Onda's own content and are not for sale.

## 2. Duration

2.2 There is no subscription and no automatic renewal.

## 3. Pricing: claims and buyouts

3.1 **Claim.** An open plot is sold at its published floor price for that plot type (in US dollars).

3.2 **Buyout.** A plot that someone already holds can be bought by anyone at **1.5 × its current value, rounded up to the next whole dollar**, and never below the floor price. The price you pay becomes the plot's new value. The previous holder's licence ends when the buyout is applied. **[LEGAL REVIEW]** Whether the displaced holder receives any refund, credit or share of the buyout. The current build gives them nothing; that must be stated prominently before purchase.

3.3 The server calculates the price. If the price changes while you are checking out (for example, someone else bought the plot first), your purchase is not applied and you are refunded (section 6).

## 4. Content approval

4.1 Every new claim and buyout is **held for a content review** before it appears on the street. Until it is approved, the plot shows a neutral "Sign in review" placeholder.

4.2 We may approve, decline or ask you to change content at our discretion. If we decline your content and you do not provide acceptable content within [LEGAL REVIEW — e.g. 7 days], we will refund you (section 6).

4.3 You give us a licence to display, resize, reformat and record your submitted name, tagline, colour and link on the Cruise and in screenshots, recordings and promotion of the Cruise for as long as your licence lasts and in archived material afterwards. **[LEGAL REVIEW]** Scope and duration of this licence.

## 6. Refunds, lost races and chargebacks

6.1 **Lost race.** If two people pay for the same plot at the same price, the first payment confirmed by Stripe wins. Any later payment is not applied and is **refunded in full** to the original payment method. We aim to do this within [LEGAL REVIEW — e.g. 5 business days]; the bank may take longer.

6.2 Payments that cannot be applied for technical reasons (amount or currency mismatch, plot not for sale) are refunded in full.

6.3 Apart from sections 4.2, 6.1 and 6.2 and where the law requires otherwise, purchases are final. **[LEGAL REVIEW]** Cooling-off rights (e.g. EU/UK 14-day right of withdrawal for digital services if we sell to those consumers), and whether the "content review then display" flow counts as performance starting.

6.4 If you file a chargeback for a payment that was applied, we may remove your content while the dispute is open. If the chargeback succeeds, your licence ends.

8.1 The Cruise is an experimental web experience. We do not guarantee any number of visitors, "drive-bys", views, clicks or sales. **Drive-bys are an anonymous, unverified counter** of how often a plot was passed on the street in a browser. They are not a verified count of people who saw the plot, they can be inaccurate, and they are not an audited advertising metric.
