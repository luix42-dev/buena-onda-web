## What creates the effect

- A long, steep-looking boulevard leads almost directly to the central vanishing point. The dense carpet of distant lights extends across the horizon and supplies most of the city's apparent scale.
- The cockpit occupies approximately the lower 40-45% of sampled frames: thin chrome wheel, amber gauges, reflective trim, a visible hood and recognizable hands. The camera largely retains its driver-eye composition in these samples.
- Warm cabin illumination contrasts with a cool teal road/sky. Dark palms and near buildings frame the vista; nearby streetlights and traffic create much stronger motion cues than the distant city.
- Glossy road highlights, light streaks and haze create atmosphere. Saturated grading and dense bright sky flecks are stylized choices, not evidence of photographic realism.
- Hand positions and near roadside objects change across consecutive samples. The small compressed image cannot establish natural finger contact, wrist articulation, steering-to-path synchronization or absence of clipping.
